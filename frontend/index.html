Frequently asked questions

Is this an official government certificate?

No. Certificates from this prototype are system-generated demonstrations and are not connected to an official government registry.

How can I check a certificate?

Open public verification and enter the certificate number or scan its QR code.

How do staff accounts become active?

LMO and GATC onboarding requires an administrator-issued invitation key, document review, and approval.
